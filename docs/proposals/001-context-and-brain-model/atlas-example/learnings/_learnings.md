# 🌟 Learnings — index

> Mother file (MOC) for `atlas/learnings/`. Career-spanning wisdom — the kind of thing a
> senior person writes when they look back on years at the firm — built continuously, day by
> day, instead of reconstructed once from memory.
> Up: [Proposal 001 — Context and Brain Model](../../001-context-and-brain-model.md), §4.

## What belongs here (and what doesn't)
- **Log it:** a genuine realization about doing this work well, leading, handling pressure,
  reading people, managing yourself — durable, not tied to one project, would still be true
  in five years.
- **Don't log it:** a case-specific decision (→ that project's Decisions subsection), a
  reusable technical method (→ [`concepts/`](../concepts/_concepts.md)), a narrow operational
  preference (→ `profile/working-preferences.md` — though a learning may graduate there once
  it becomes a standing practice).

## How it's fed
- **Immediate capture:** the moment a standout insight surfaces mid-conversation, filed the
  same way any durable fact would be under retrieval-first.
- **Weekly retro (primary):** the retro explicitly distills durable learnings out of the
  week's `daily/` evidence — the main engine that fills this file over time, not an
  afterthought bolted onto the retro ritual.
