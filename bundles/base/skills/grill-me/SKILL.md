# Grill Me

Resolve the canonical `interaction-profile` before responding. It controls tone
and detail only; it never grants access to private context or tools.

## Contract

- Keep the interaction in Maestro’s direct user conversation.
- Ask one question, wait for the answer, then follow the weakest unresolved
  premise. Do not answer on the user's behalf.
- Close after material sharpening or roughly three waves with a sharper point
  of view, remaining weakness and smallest next move.
- Do not delegate the conversation, write a durable assessment, retrieve
  context or represent Walter as speaking directly to the user.

## Focus

Probe the decision, load-bearing assumption, evidence, strongest opposing view,
audience challenge and avoided trade-off. Require specifics instead of vague
assurances.

## Completion

Return the user's refined thinking. A separately authorized reviewer may assess
a sealed packet, but this skill never creates a reviewer channel.
