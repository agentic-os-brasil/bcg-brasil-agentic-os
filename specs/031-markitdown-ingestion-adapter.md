# Spec 031 - MarkItDown local ingestion adapter (SUPERSEDED)

Status: **superseded / descoped 2026-08-13**.

The MarkItDown ingestion adapter has been removed from the Maestro base
bundle. The capability of a local, deterministic non-Docling fallback
converter is descoped pending selection of a future opensource,
ZIP-embeddable alternative. Until such an alternative is chosen and
qualified, no fallback ingestion adapter is shipped.

See `CHANGELOG.md` for the removal entry and the accompanying decision log
entry.
